# **ADIDAS Sales Analysis**

## **📌 Project Description**
This project analyzes retail sales data to gain valuable Adidas business insights. The dataset contains transaction records, including information on retailers, products, pricing, sales, profits, and regional performance. The goal is to identify sales trends, profitability, and key business drivers using SQL queries and Power BI visualizations.

## ** Sources **
[Sources](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/heemalichaudhari/adidas-sales-dataset)


## **🔹 Objectives**
- Understand sales performance over time and across regions.
- Identify top-selling products and most profitable categories.
- Compare online vs. in-store sales trends.
- Analyze revenue growth and sales efficiency.
- Provide actionable insights through Power BI visualizations.

## **📂 Dataset Overview**
The dataset consists of the following columns:
- **Retailer**: Name of the retailer.
- **Retailer ID**: Unique identifier for the retailer.
- **Invoice Date**: Date of the transaction.
- **Region**: Sales region.
- **State**: State where the sale occurred.
- **City**: City where the sale occurred.
- **Product**: Name of the product sold.
- **Price per Unit**: Price of a single unit of the product.
- **Units Sold**: Number of units sold.
- **Total Sales**: Total revenue generated from the sale.
- **Operating Profit**: Profit earned after deducting operational costs.
- **Operating Margin**: Profitability ratio.
- **Sales Method**: Sales channel (e.g., In-store, Online).

## **📊 SQL Analysis Questions**
The following SQL-based analyses help answer critical business questions:

### **1️⃣ Sales & Revenue Analysis**
- **Total Sales Per Retailer** – Identify which retailer generates the highest revenue.
- **Best-Selling Product Categories** – Find out which products have the highest demand.
- **Daily Revenue Trend** – Analyze how sales change on a daily basis.
- **Most Profitable State** – Determine which states contribute the most profit.
- **Top-Selling Cities** – Identify the cities with the highest total revenue.

### **2️⃣ Performance & Efficiency Analysis**
- **Average Profit Margin Per Product** – Understand which products yield the highest profitability.
- **In-Store vs. Online Sales Comparison** – Compare revenue generated from different sales methods.
- **Revenue Growth Over Time** – Track monthly revenue trends.

## **📈 Power BI Data Visualizations**
To present the insights effectively, the following Power BI visualizations are used:

1. **Sales Performance Over Time** (Line Chart) – Shows sales trends over different time periods.
2. **Top-Selling Products** (Bar Chart) – Highlights the most popular products based on units sold.
3. **Sales by Region & State** (Map Visualization) – Displays geographical sales distribution.
4. **Profitability by Product** (Treemap) – Identifies the most profitable product categories.
5. **Sales Method Comparison** (Pie Chart) – Compares sales between different channels.
6. **Monthly Sales Trend** (Area Chart) – Tracks revenue trends over months.
7. **Revenue by City** (Bar Chart) – Ranks cities by total sales revenue.
