# **Retail Sales Dashboard - Power BI**

## 📌 **Project Overview**
This Power BI dashboard provides insights into retail sales data, helping businesses analyze sales trends and customer behavior. The dashboard consists of two main pages:
1. **Sales Overview** - A high-level summary of total sales performance.
2. **Customer Insights** - A deep dive into customer demographics and spending patterns.

## 📊 **Dataset Description**
The dataset includes transaction records with the following columns:
- **invoice_no**: Unique transaction ID
- **customer_id**: Unique customer ID
- **gender**: Customer gender (Male/Female)
- **age**: Customer age
- **category**: Product category (Clothing, Shoes, etc.)
- **quantity**: Number of items purchased
- **price**: Price per unit
- **payment_method**: Mode of payment (Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash)
- **invoice_date**: Date of transaction
- **shopping_mall**: Location where the purchase was made

## 📈 **Dashboard Pages & Visualizations**

### 🟢 **1. Sales Overview (Main Page)**
💡 **Purpose:** Provide a high-level summary of key sales metrics.

🔹 **Visualizations:**
- **Total Revenue & Total Sales** (Card Visuals)
- **Sales by Category** (Bar Chart)
- **Sales Trend Over Time** (Line Chart)
- **Top 5 Shopping Malls by Sales** (Stacked Column Chart)
- **Sales by Payment Method** (Pie Chart)

📌 **Filters/Slicers:**
- Date range selector
- Category filter
- Shopping mall filter

---

### 🟡 **2. Customer Insights Page**
💡 **Purpose:** Understand customer demographics and behavior.

🔹 **Visualizations:**
- **Customer Age Distribution** (Histogram)
- **Sales by Gender** (Pie Chart)
- **Average Spending per Customer** (Card Visual)
- **Top Customers by Total Spend** (Table with Conditional Formatting)
- **Sales by Customer Age Group** (Bar Chart)

📌 **Filters/Slicers:**
- Gender filter
- Age group filter

---

## 🚀 **How to Use the Dashboard**
1. **Open Power BI Desktop** and load the dataset (CSV/Excel format).
2. **Use the provided visuals and slicers** to interact with the data.
3. **Filter data dynamically** by selecting date ranges, categories, and shopping malls.
4. **Analyze trends** in customer spending and sales performance.
